Orbfall privacy

Last updated 12 November 2026

Orbfall is a small game made by an independent developer. It has no accounts and asks for no email address, phone number or location. This page says what the game keeps, what it sends, and what the ad service does.

What stays on your device

Your scores, your settings, the name you typed and the run in progress are saved in your browser's storage for this site, so the game can pick up where you left off. They are not sent anywhere except as described below. Clearing this site's data in your browser erases all of it.

The online ranking

If the online board is switched on in settings, the game sends your best scores to the ranking. Each entry holds:

  • the name you typed,
  • a random player number the game made up on your device, which is not tied to you or to the device,
  • the score, the largest size you reached, the seed of that run, whether rescues were used, and the time.

The ranking is public: other players see the name and the score. To slow down floods, the ranking keeps a scrambled form of the sender's internet address for two minutes and then deletes it. Turn off the online board in settings and nothing more is sent. To have an entry removed, write to the address below with the name on the board.

Advertising

The game only shows an ad when you choose one, to undo moves or rescue a run. Ads are provided by Google. When an ad is requested:

  • Google and its advertising partners may use cookies or similar identifiers on your device to choose and measure ads, including ads based on your earlier visits to this and other sites.
  • You can see how Google uses information from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
  • You can switch off personalised advertising from Google at adssettings.google.com, and from many other companies at aboutads.info/choices.
  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, you are asked for your choice before any personalised ad is shown, and you can change it later from the same message.

The game itself gives the ad service nothing about you: not your name, your scores or your player number.

Who else handles data

  • The game's files are served by GitHub Pages and the ranking runs on Cloudflare. Like any web host, both receive your internet address when your browser asks for a page, and keep their own short-lived logs.
  • Google, for the ads described above.

There are no analytics or tracking tools in the game beyond these.

Children

Orbfall is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes and contact

If the game starts keeping or sending anything else, this page will say so first, with a new date at the top.
