Two of a kind merge. Stack past the line and the run ends.

How it works

Drop an orb. Two of a kind touching merge into the next size; three touching at once skip a size and score double. Merges within a second of each other chain for a bonus. Stack past the line for a second and the run ends.

    Every 5,000 points the next piece is a black hole: drop it and it eats the whole board, scoring every orb. Rush drops the piece when its clock runs out. Undo and the rescues at game over mark the run on the board; a run without them earns ✦.
